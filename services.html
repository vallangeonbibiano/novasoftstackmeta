// 01 — Audio Synthesis

Programmatic Audio Synthesis

Generative sound engines that produce infinite, organic soundscapes from algorithmic primitives. No audio files. No large downloads. Just pure mathematical beauty rendered in real time on the device.

A · 01

Compact wavetable oscillators with smooth morphing across complex timbres. Perfect for UI feedback, ambient layers, and music applications.

  • Sub-millisecond latency

A · 02

  • Modulation matrix

  • Crossfade orchestration

// 05 — Ad Monetization

Programmatic Monetization,
Globally Compliant.

We integrate and manage the full IAA & IAP stack — from mediation layers to ad-format strategy to fraud prevention — across 40+ ad networks. Open RTB bidding, waterfalls, header bidding, ATT, COPPA, kid-safe modes — all wired correctly from day one.

Ad Formats We Implement

Splash / App Open

Full-screen impression shown at app cold-launch or foreground return. Best for brand awareness and high-CPM direct deals. We configure timing, frequency caps, and skip logic for compliance.

Rewarded Video

User-initiated video ads that unlock in-app rewards. Highest eCPM format. We implement skip-after-5-second logic, completion tracking, reward fulfillment, and attribution. Highest Yield

Interstitial

Full-screen ads shown at natural transition points (level complete, between content). Frequency capping, cool-downs, and pre-load optimization for zero-latency display. High Fill

Banner

Always-visible, lightweight ad units. Standard, large, medium, and custom sizes. Adaptive banners for dynamic screen handling. Refresh logic and viewability optimization.

Native

Customizable ad templates that match your app's visual design. Perfect for content feeds, list items, and editorial placements. Higher CTR, better UX, premium eCPMs.

Playable / Interactive

Mini-game ad units that let users try before they install. Highest engagement, premium eCPM. We implement clickable previews, end-cards, and store redirects.
